The BIMA Doctor evaluates symptoms and determines the necessary medical pathway:

Informed Consent & Tele-health Limitations

Tele-health involves healthcare delivery via interactive audio/video technology where patient and doctor are in different locations. While tele-health offers convenience and accessibility, subscribers acknowledge potential inherent limitations:

  • Information Quality: Poor call audio or low image resolution may occasionally hinder complete clinical evaluation.
  • Technical Delays: Network or electronic system failures outside BIMA's control may disrupt service availability.
  • Medical History Gaps: Incomplete disclosures or unavailable past medical records may increase risk of drug interactions or diagnostic limitations.
  • Security: While robust encryption protocols are enforced, no electronic transmission over public networks can guarantee absolute immunity from security breaches.
  • Doctor Discretion: BIMA Doctors reserve the right to determine that tele-health is unsuitable for specific conditions and direct patients to physical facilities.
