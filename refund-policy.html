Refund Policy

BIMA aims to provide a transparent and fair payment experience to its customers. This Refund Policy explains when and how a customer may request a refund for payments made for BIMA products and services.

Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the nature of the product or service purchased, the reason for the refund request, the applicable product Terms & Conditions, and applicable laws and regulations.

A refund may be considered in circumstances including, but not limited to:

  • An incorrect or duplicate payment.
  • A payment for a service that was not successfully activated.
  • A transaction that is otherwise determined by BIMA to be eligible for a refund under the applicable product terms.

Certain products, subscriptions, services, or insurance products may have specific refund, cancellation, or cooling-off provisions. Customers should refer to the applicable product Terms & Conditions.

🚫

Non-Refundable Payments

Unless otherwise stated in the applicable product Terms & Conditions or required under applicable law, payments may not be refundable where:

  • The applicable service has already been successfully activated or delivered.
  • The customer has used the service or benefits associated with the payment.
  • The refund request does not meet the applicable product's refund or cancellation conditions.
  • The payment relates to a service for which the applicable terms specifically exclude refunds.

To help us process a refund request, customers may be asked to provide:

  • Transaction or subscription details.
  • Date and amount of the transaction.
  • Reason for requesting the refund.
  • Relevant payment or transaction reference, where available.

BIMA may request additional information where required to verify the transaction and assess the refund request.

🔍

Refund Review

All refund requests are subject to verification.

BIMA will review the transaction and determine whether the payment is eligible for a refund based on the applicable product terms, transaction records, customer authorization/consent records, service activation or usage records, and applicable laws and regulations.

Submitting a refund request does not automatically guarantee a refund.

⚙️

Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, BIMA will process the refund through the applicable payment method or payment channel, where technically and operationally possible.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account may vary depending on the customer's bank, mobile wallet, card issuer, or other payment service provider.

BIMA is not responsible for delays caused by third-party payment providers after the refund has been successfully initiated by BIMA.

🔄

Subscription Cancellation

Where applicable, customers may request cancellation of a recurring subscription in accordance with the relevant product Terms & Conditions.

Cancellation of a subscription does not automatically result in a refund of previously paid amounts. Any refund will be assessed based on the applicable product-specific refund and cancellation terms.

🛡️

Insurance Products

For insurance products, refunds, cancellations, and premium adjustments will be handled in accordance with the applicable insurance policy terms, applicable regulatory requirements, and arrangements between the relevant parties.

Where a specific insurance product has separate cancellation or refund provisions, those provisions will apply.

⚠️ Disclaimer

This policy should be read together with the applicable product Terms & Conditions. Where product-specific terms provide different refund or cancellation conditions, those terms will apply, subject to applicable law.
